ElectroField — electromagnetics you can check

Interactive chapters for electrical and electronics engineers, following Ulaby & Ravaioli and Cheng. A Python engine (NumPy/SciPy) computes every quantity, writes out the derivation with the numbers substituted, and verifies each result by an independent method.

  • 1-D, 2-D and 3-D waves
  • Vectors and coordinate systems
  • Electrostatics: Coulomb, Gauss, capacitance, images
  • Magnetostatics: Biot–Savart, Ampère, inductance
  • Maxwell's equations and boundary conditions
  • Plane waves in lossy media, skin depth
  • Reflection, transmission, Brewster angle
  • Transmission lines and the Smith chart
  • Waveguides and antennas
  • Question bank
  • EM tutor (AI)
  • Verification report
